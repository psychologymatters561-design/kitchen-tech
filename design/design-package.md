# Design package: "Ek Rasoi" (one kitchen, one walk)

Tier 3, choreographed site. The film IS the website's opening: every chapter of the film is a section of the page, and the page below lives in the same kitchen, same light, same grade.

## Producer
- Model: Seedance 2.5, image-to-video with start AND end frames (so the three shots join with no seam), 1080p, 6 s each, no audio.
- Cost: 3 shots x 96 = 288 credits. Key frames: 4 images x 0.25 = 1 credit. Total about 290 of 600. Leaves about 310 = three retakes.
- Real assets: Aanch product photo (infrared-cooker.png, 310x247, carries a third-party logo on its panel, so it is used as a shape reference only, never shown raw), Kitchen Tech glass-top gas stove (LOTUS-3.png), about-us banner (logo, brass burners). All 49 original photos go into the lower sections via the build.
- Mobile: phones see the designed still (the settle frame) with captions, not the scrubbing video. Desktop and laptop get the full scrub. The Aanch page keeps its drawn coil film, which runs smoothly on phones.
- Disclosure: "Film made with AI. The product is real." under the film.

## Researcher
- Buyers' words: cylinder khatam, booking nahi ho rahi, 25 din ka gap, induction pe bartan nahi chalte, bill kitna aayega, roti phoolti hai kya.
- The ONE call to action: Buy Aanch (cash on delivery), WhatsApp as the safety net.

## Storyboard (one continuous first-person walk, about 18 s of scroll)

World grade for every frame: Indian home kitchen at dusk. Warm tungsten practicals, cool blue window light, brass and steel catching highlights, dark slate counter, terracotta and old teak. Palette: tawa black #14100d, atta #f4ede2, aanch ember #e8461f, glow #ffb25e, brass #c49a56. No people, no hands, no text, no logos.
