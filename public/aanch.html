    Questions

    Asked before buying.

    Is it cheaper than gas?

    It depends on two numbers: your cylinder price and your electricity rate. With a ₹942 home cylinder, Aanch is cheaper when your power costs less than about ₹6.2 a unit, and costs a little more above that. With a commercial cylinder or a black-market refill, Aanch is clearly cheaper. Try the calculator with your own numbers.

    How much electricity does it use?

    At full power, a 2,000 W cooktop uses 2 units an hour. The coil switches on and off to hold the heat you set, so normal cooking uses less than that. A family that cooks everything on it uses roughly 100 to 120 units a month. This model's exact wattage: .

    Which bartan can I use?

    Any bartan with a flat bottom: steel, aluminium, copper, brass, cast iron, glass, clay and ceramic. Flat bottoms heat fastest. A round-bottom kadai still works, it just takes a little longer.

    Can I really make roti on the glass?

    Yes. Put the rolled roti straight on the hot glass, flip once, and it puffs up like it does on a flame. Wipe off any flour once the glass has cooled.

    Is it safe? Does the glass get hot?

    There is no flame and no gas, so nothing can leak. The glass does get very hot while cooking and stays hot for a few minutes after you switch off, so keep little hands away until it cools. Safety features on this model: .

    Will it work in a power cut?

    No. It needs mains power. Most home inverters cannot run a 2,000 W cooktop. That's why we suggest keeping your gas stove for power cuts and using Aanch the rest of the time.

    What socket do I need?

    A cooktop this powerful needs a heavy-duty 16 A power socket, the bigger one your geyser or AC uses, not the small 6 A one. Model detail: .
